Core Privacy Commitment: OmniScribe uses an on-device Local AI Neural Engine. Your audio, video, transcripts, translated text, and exported files are processed locally on your device. We never upload your media or transcripts to any server, and we never receive them.

1. Overview

Total Soft Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") developed OmniScribe, an on-device AI transcription and translation app. OmniScribe is free to use with advertisements, and offers an optional paid upgrade (OmniScribe Pro) that removes ads.

This policy explains what information is processed on your device, what limited technical data is handled by third-party services the app relies on, and the choices you have.

2. On-Device Processing (Your Content)

The following operations take place entirely on your device. The content involved never leaves your phone:

  • Media Transcription: Audio and video files you choose are converted to text locally by the app's neural engine. Media files are read only from the files you select and are never uploaded.
  • Offline Translation: Transcript translation and language detection run on-device using Google ML Kit. Your text is not sent to Google or anyone else.
  • Hardware Benchmarking: Device capability tests run locally to recommend a suitable AI model for your phone.
  • Document Export: TXT and PDF files are generated locally and saved where you choose. Exported files remain under your control.
  • Local History & Settings: Saved transcripts, processing statistics, and app preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

OmniScribe does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or precise location permissions.

3. Downloads the App Makes

Some features require a one-time download. These downloads send only standard technical request information (such as your IP address and device/network details needed to deliver the file). No audio, video, or transcript content is ever sent.

  • AI Models: Transcription models are downloaded on your request from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a third-party model-hosting service, using Android's system download manager. Once downloaded, models run fully offline.
  • Translation Language Packs: When you first translate into a language, Google ML Kit downloads that language pack from Google servers. After that, translation into that language works offline.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Collected

To keep OmniScribe free and stable, the app uses the following Google services. They collect limited technical data, never your media or transcripts:

Service Purpose Data Collected
Google AdMob Banner and optional rewarded ads (free users only) Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions
Firebase Analytics Understanding feature usage to improve the app App usage events (e.g. screens opened, features used), app instance ID, device model, OS version, approximate location derived from IP address
Firebase Crashlytics Diagnosing crashes and bugs Crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, crash-related identifiers
Google Play Billing Processing OmniScribe Pro purchases Purchase status and purchase token. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment information.
Google User Messaging Platform Showing ad-consent choices where required by law Your consent choices and region-related information

Data collected by these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with Google only as needed to provide the services above, under Google's own privacy policies.

5. Advertising Choices & Consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other applicable regions): Where required, OmniScribe asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices inside the app.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: You choose whether to watch an ad before starting a transcription. You can upgrade to Pro to remove ads entirely.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some devices).
  • Pro users: Ads are not requested for OmniScribe Pro users.

6. Permissions Used

  • Internet & network state: Downloading AI models and translation packs, ads, analytics, crash reports, and purchases.
  • Notifications: Showing progress of long transcriptions and downloads.
  • Foreground service & wake lock: Letting a transcription finish while the screen is off or the app is in the background.
  • Storage (Android 9 only): Saving exported files on older devices.
  • Billing: Enabling in-app purchases through Google Play.

7. Device Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, your app settings and transcript history may be included in your personal Google account backup, managed by Android and Google. Downloaded AI models are excluded from backups. You can turn off backup in your device settings.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your content: Clear transcript history at any time in the app (Settings or History). Uninstalling OmniScribe permanently deletes all local data, transcripts, and downloaded models from your device.
  • Analytics and crash data: Retained by Google according to Firebase's standard retention periods. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your app installation, contact us at the email below.
  • Purchase records: Kept by Google Play as part of your Google account purchase history.

9. Children's Privacy

OmniScribe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.
