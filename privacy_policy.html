Core Privacy Commitment: OmniScribe uses an on-device Local AI Neural Engine. Your audio, video, transcripts, translated text, and exported files are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your media or transcripts to cloud transcription servers.

1. Overview & Architecture

Total Soft Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") developed OmniScribe as an ad-supported mobile application. This policy details how data is handled when you use our software.

OmniScribe operates as an edge-AI utility. Unlike cloud transcription apps, speech processing and document translation execute directly on your physical device hardware.

2. On-Device Processing

The following operations take place entirely on your device:

  • Media Transcription: Imported audio and video recordings are processed locally by the app's neural engine. Media files never leave your device.
  • Local Translation: Text translation of transcripts uses local on-device resources.
  • Hardware Benchmarking: Device capability testing runs locally to suggest optimal processing parameters for your hardware.
  • Document Export: Generating TXT or PDF files with optional timestamps is executed locally. Exported files remain under your personal control.
  • Local History & Storage: Saved transcripts and application preferences are stored inside local application memory. You can clear history or reset app storage at any time.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Data

To support free access and maintain application stability, OmniScribe integrates standard developer SDKs that require internet connectivity for specific non-media operational tasks:

A. Display & Rewarded Advertisements

OmniScribe uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded ads. Ad networks may collect non-personal technical identifiers, including:

  • Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) and device hardware specs.
  • Approximate IP-based location and ad interaction analytics.

B. Anonymized Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose bugs. In the event of an application crash, diagnostic data is collected, including stack traces, device model, and OS version. Crash reports never contain your media files or transcript text.

5. Data Retention & Control

You can clear your local history and deleted data within the app settings. Uninstalling OmniScribe permanently wipes all local databases, saved transcripts, and cached configuration data from your phone.
